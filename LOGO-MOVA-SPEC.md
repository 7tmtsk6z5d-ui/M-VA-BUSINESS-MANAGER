# MŌVA Logo — Brand Lockup

The final foundation build uses a geometric M monogram based on the supplied written specification:
- deep navy `#0A1628`
- two stable outer vertical legs
- controlled internal diagonal fold
- strong negative space and small-size recognition
- flat vector construction
- MŌVA wordmark with visible macron on Ō

Assets:
- `assets/logo-mark.svg` — standalone app monogram
- `assets/mova-lockup.svg` — horizontal MŌVA lockup
- `assets/icon-192.png` — PWA icon
- `assets/icon-512.png` — PWA icon

The primary logo is intentionally flat; metallic/bevel effects are reserved for optional future splash-screen treatments and are not part of the core brand mark.
